## Table S3

Predicted MHC Class I and II binding epitopes identified using NetMHCpan and NetMHCIIpan. Positions (Pos) correspond to amino acid residue numbers within the NS1 protein sequence. "MHC" and "Locus" indicate the predicted allele and reference dataset used by NetMHCIIpan, respectively. "Peptide" corresponds to the complete predicted epitope sequence, whereas "Core" denotes the minimal binding motif identified by NetMHCpan. The remaining parameters correspond to prediction metrics generated by NetMHCpan and NetMHCIIpan, including eluted-ligand likelihood score (Score_EL), binding affinity score (Score_BA), percentile ranks (%Rank_EL and %Rank_BA), and predicted binding affinity (Aff, nM). Predicted binders were classified as strong binders (SB) when ranked within the top 0.5 % according to Score_EL and as weak binders (WB) when ranked within the top 2% and 5% according to NetMHCpan and NetMHCIIpan, respectively.
